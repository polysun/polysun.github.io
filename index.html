Profile

Researching intelligence from the perspective of the person.

I study how people perceive, understand, and act with intelligent systems—and how interaction can make those systems more useful to each individual.

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Psychology and Cognitive Science at East China Normal University and Director of the Personal Informatics System Lab. I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Beyond the lab

I play, collect, travel, and talk ideas through.

Research occupies most of my week, but not all of it. I make time for turn-based strategy, creature-collecting, open-ended exploration, platform and party games, as well as collectible card games. I enjoy systems that reveal themselves gradually—rules learned by doing, small choices that become stories, and people learning from one another.

I also enjoy making podcasts and hosting live streams outside work. Speaking to an audience is a different kind of interaction: ideas have to become clearer, questions arrive from unexpected directions, and conversation develops in real time.

Travel, photography, and time near the sea help me slow down and notice things again. These interests are not research assignments, but they do return to the work—in how I think about play, feedback, participation, curiosity, and the many ways people make a system their own.

Research

From human state
to human capability.

My work connects sensing, behavioral evidence, computational models, and interaction design. The objective is not intelligence in isolation, but intelligence that helps a particular person understand, act, and flourish.

R1

Human state
& cognition

Measure perception, attention, affect, cognitive load, and physiological signals in real tasks.

R2

Interaction
methodology

Build tangible, multimodal, and adaptive interactions that make system intent perceivable and negotiable.

R3

Embodied
& physical AI

Understand physical and embodied intelligence from the human side: trust, coordination, legibility, and shared control.

Research loop

  1. Prototype
  2. Observe
  3. Model
  4. Personalize
