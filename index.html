00 Profile

Making powerful intelligence useful to individual people.

I study the person inside an intelligent system: what people perceive, how they decide, where interaction breaks down, and how a system can adapt without taking control away.

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Psychology and Cognitive Science at East China Normal University and Director of the Personal Informatics System Lab. I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

01 Research

From human state
to human capability.

My work connects sensing, behavioral evidence, computational models, and interaction design. The objective is not intelligence in isolation, but intelligence that helps a particular person understand, act, and flourish.

R / 01

Human state
& cognition

Measure perception, attention, affect, cognitive load, and physiological signals in real tasks.

R / 02

Interaction
methodology

Build tangible, multimodal, and adaptive interactions that make system intent perceivable and negotiable.

R / 03

Embodied
& physical AI

Understand physical and embodied intelligence from the human side: trust, coordination, legibility, and shared control.
